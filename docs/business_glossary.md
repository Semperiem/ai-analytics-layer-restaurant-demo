# Business Glossary

## Revenue
Total sales amount before refunds, calculated from order item quantity multiplied by selling price.

## Gross margin
Revenue minus estimated product cost.

## Average order value (AOV)
Revenue divided by number of orders.

## Repeat customer
A customer with more than one completed order in the selected period.

## Churn risk
A customer-level indicator based on recency, frequency, and declining order behavior.

## Channel
The order source: dine-in, delivery, app, web, or phone.

## Same-store sales
Revenue comparison for stores active in both comparison periods.
